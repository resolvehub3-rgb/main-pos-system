Privacy Policy

SamTill — a product of SamTeck Digital · Effective 28 October 2026

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains what information SamTeck Digital handles when you use the SamTill website (accounts, payment, installer downloads) and when your shop runs the SamTill point-of-sale application. We do not sell personal data and we do not run advertising or tracking cookies.

2. This website

The marketing pages of the site are static. They carry no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Any message you compose in the contact section is opened in your own email or WhatsApp application from your own device — it is not collected or stored by this website.

3. Your customer account

When you register to buy or download SamTill we handle:

  • your email address, and whether you have verified it;
  • your sign-in session, which is kept in your own browser so you stay signed in;
  • your orders — the package bought, amount, payment reference and payment status;
  • installer download records (which release, when), kept for security and support.

We send only transactional email about your account and purchase (verification, purchase confirmation, download help). We do not add you to a marketing list without asking.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Paystack. Your mobile money number, card details and PIN are entered on Paystack's own payment page and are never sent to, or stored by, this website or the SamTill application. We receive only the payment outcome, amount, method and reference needed to activate your licence.

5. Data your shop enters in SamTill

The SamTill application runs on your own computer. Your products, prices, sales, stock, staff and expense records are stored on that computer and are also mirrored to a secure cloud database so that your other tills can show the same shop data and so the data can be recovered if the computer fails.

  • Access is scoped by shop: each shop's records are separated from every other shop's.
  • Your customer-facing display shows only what you choose to show on it.
  • Cashiers only see what their role allows — owner-only areas such as stock valuation, expenses and staff management are restricted.
  • Receipts print locally through a small program on your own till machine; nothing is sent to us when you print.

6. Who we share data with

  • Paystack — to take and verify payments.
  • Supabase — our database and email-delivery infrastructure, under contract, for account and shop data.
  • Our email provider — to send the verification and purchase emails described above.

We disclose data only where the law requires it, or where needed to investigate fraud or a disputed payment.

7. How long we keep it

Account and order records are kept while your account is open and for as long as we must keep financial records under Ghanaian law. Shop data stays in your cloud backup for as long as your licence is active. Ask us to delete your account and shop data and we will do so, except where we must retain records for tax, accounting or a live dispute.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Database access is restricted by row-level security so one shop cannot read another's records, and cloud access uses service credentials that are never shipped to your computer. No system is perfectly secure, so we also rely on you to keep your sign-in details private.

9. Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Use the contact details below and we will respond within a reasonable period. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially we will update it here with a new effective date. Questions: use the contact section of this website, or write to SamTeck Digital, Mamprobi, Accra, Ghana.
